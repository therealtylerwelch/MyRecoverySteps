The first half was on plan. Through Olympian Hall at mile 51 she was within ~10 minutes of this 32-hour schedule — actually ahead of it at Long Lake, Billy's, and Dry Lake. The engine is there.
2
The night loop broke it. The Emerald Mountain out-and-back (Lane of Pain, miles 51–64) took nearly five hours at 22.9 min/mi. She left Olympian tracking a 32-hour finish and came back about 1h20 behind.
3
Read the fade in her pace, not her heart rate. After dark the HR trace goes unreliable — it reads in the 40s–50s while she's climbing steep grades (see the chart below), a cold- and loose-strap sensor dropout, not a real cardiac crash. The solid signal is pace, and it ballooned past 20 min/mi. Tellingly, the cold that fooled the watch points straight at the real problem: the temperature fell from a 68°F afternoon to the low 40s through the night, bound for a 34°F dawn — she was getting genuinely cold.
4
The clock wasn't the enemy. She reached mile 64 at 20:11 — with more than 15 hours still on the 36-hour cutoff to cover the last 38 miles. She had the time. Staying warm, fueled, and moving through the night was the fight.
Redemption plan: protect the night. Layer up and fuel hard before sunset, pick up a pacer at Olympian (allowed both directions), keep the Emerald loop moving, and get in and out of Olympian fast — she spent ~27 minutes there on the way out. The last 38 miles are the ones to go get.

How to read this

Move is the running time for the leg into that aid station; Rest is the time spent there (type a new number to change it). Arrive is the clock time she reaches the station, Elapsed her total race time so far. Most stations appear twice — the OUT / BACK tag says which pass; EMER is the Emerald Mountain loop (Lane of Pain, out and back).

Crew access: a ◆ CREW badge means crew may meet her there; ◇ PACER means a pacer may join or leave. Fish Creek Falls allows crew on foot or bike only (no vehicles); Billy's Rabbit Hole and Lane of Pain are minimal aid with no crew.

Cutoff cushion is how far ahead of the official time limit she is — green comfortable, amber tight, red at or behind. A dash means no cutoff at that stop. This is a planning estimate; use the cushion, not the exact minute, to make calls on the day.

1st Attempt ’23 is Shannon's actual elapsed time at each aid station from her 2023 Garmin file, with a chip showing how it compared to this plan (− ahead, + / + behind).
