How to read this

Move is the running time for the leg into that aid station; Rest is the time spent there (type a new number to change it). Arrive is the clock time she reaches the station, Elapsed her total race time so far. Most stations appear twice — the OUT / BACK tag says which pass; EMER is the Emerald Mountain loop (Lane of Pain, out and back).

Crew access: a ◆ CREW badge means crew may meet her there; ◇ PACER means a pacer may join or leave. Fish Creek Falls allows crew on foot or bike only (no vehicles); Billy's Rabbit Hole and Lane of Pain are minimal aid with no crew.

Cutoff cushion is how far ahead of the official time limit she is — green comfortable, amber tight, red at or behind. A dash means no cutoff at that stop. This is a planning estimate; use the cushion, not the exact minute, to make calls on the day.
