⚠ The one thing that decides this race: protect the night

In 2023 Shannon was on 32-hour pace to halfway, then the Emerald Mountain loop (miles 51–64) unraveled it — solo, in 29°F feels-like cold. This year Jason joins at Olympian on the return (mile 64), so she runs that same loop solo again.
That makes the mile-51 Olympian stop the most important crew stop of the race: send her into the loop fully warm, fueled, caffeinated, and lit — nobody is with her until she climbs back to Olympian at mile 64. Warm her before she's cold, not after.
CREW ACCESSNo crew vehicles on Buffalo Pass Rd above Dry Lake. Fish Creek Falls is foot/bike pack-in only — no driving. Summit Lake is carpool/drop-off only.
MANDATORY GEARNew for 2026 — Shannon may not leave Olympian Hall without: headlamp + backup light, warm jacket, space blanket, raincoat, gloves. Crew verifies this at both Olympian stops.
PACERAs a Tortoise, Shannon may have one pacer from Olympian (mile 51) to the finish. Jason paces until Summit Lake return (mile 80.8), where Tyler takes over crew.
